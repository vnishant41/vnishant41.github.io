Automated Data Pipeline

IPGA Data Management & Daily Price CSV Conversion Sheet

Standardizing, entity-mapping, and automating daily price feeds from the India Pulses and Grains Association (IPGA) into structured, database-ready datasets.

The Data Challenge

Raw IPGA daily feeds contain varying unit formats (Rs/20kg, 50kg bags, trucks, $/MT, Kyats), regional mandi name spellings (e.g. Gulbarga vs Kalaburagi), and non-standard variety descriptions across domestic and international trade centers.

Unstructured Feeds Multi-Unit Noise Entity Mismatch

The Automated Solution

Developed an automated Daily Price CSV Conversion Sheet using Excel, VBA, and Power Query that validates incoming feeds, maps local mandi names to standard database entities, normalizes rates to standard quintals/tonnes, and outputs clean CSV tables.
