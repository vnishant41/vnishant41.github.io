PROACTIVE ORGANIZATIONAL INITIATIVE · DATA PIPELINE AUTOMATION

IPGA Daily Price CSV Conversion Workflow

Excel / VBA / Data Engineering

Engineered an automated data management workflow for daily market price feeds from the India Pulses and Grains Association (IPGA). Built proactively beyond core daily duties, the system standardizes heterogeneous raw feeds into clean, database-ready CSV datasets.

The Problem

Raw feeds contained unstandardized mandi spellings (e.g. Gulbarga vs Kalaburagi), mixed trade units (Rs/20kg, 50kg bags, $/MT, Kyats), and varying nomenclatures.

The Solution

Developed an automated Excel conversion sheet utilizing VBA macros, entity mapping matrices, and validation rules to normalize units and standardize varieties.

The Outcome

Eliminated manual formatting delays, prevented entity duplication, and produced clean daily CSV exports for Domestic Spot Rates, Arrivals, and C&F Prices.
